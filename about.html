BACKGROUND & PHILOSOPHY

How I Build Products

Great product managers are not feature administrators. They are systems thinkers who connect user psychology, technical architecture, and unit economics to drive business outcomes.

Ghanshyam Kumar Singh · Product Manager

I didn't start my career writing PRDs or building roadmaps. I started by talking to users.

My first product role involved conducting over 2,000 qualitative user discovery calls with real players across India. That experience taught me something that still drives how I build products today: users will rarely tell you the solution, but they will always show you where your assumptions fail.

From user discovery, I took ownership of lifecycle communication, segmentation, and retention across 50M+ registered users. That evolved into managing game economies, pricing, and live-ops for products generating ₹8L/day. Along the way, I solved unglamorous platform problems — rebuilding identity verification pipelines to <1 minute, navigating emergency regulatory transitions within 10 days, and architecting in-house analytics engines on Kafka and ClickHouse.

Today, I build 0-to-1 GenAI products at AIVO Technologies, leading an 8-member cross-functional pod shipping multilingual voice agents, conversational analytics, and autonomous telephony workflows.

Methodology

My Product Operating System

A repeatable framework for moving from ambiguous customer signals to durable commercial outcomes.

01

Discover

Talk directly to users (2,000+ interviews), analyze behavioral drop-offs, inspect database logs, benchmark competitors, and map the underlying economic system.

02

Frame

Separate superficial symptoms from root problems. Define the core user friction, operational constraints, and commercial north-star before drafting specs.

03

Prioritize

Apply RICE scoring and willingness-to-pay evidence to ruthlessly focus on the smallest viable MVP rather than building bloated feature wishlists.

04

Experiment

Formulate clear hypotheses with explicit leading indicators (session depth, occupancy) and lagging indicators (GGR, ARPU) across controlled live A/B cohorts.

05

Ship & Learn

Deploy with comprehensive telemetry, monitor edge-case exceptions, conduct retrospectives, and double down on validated drivers.

Strategic Judgement

How I Make Product Trade-offs

Strong product management is defined by what you choose not to build. Real trade-offs and the signals that drove them.

Voice vs Chat Automation

Prioritized voice for MVP instead of web chat

Signal: 200+ user discovery calls revealed small businesses had ~3x higher willingness-to-pay for voice calling because unanswered phone calls meant direct lost revenue.

AI Support Sequencing

Empowered agents with AI before automating customers

Signal: Agent knowledge was the true bottleneck, not channel capacity. Deploying an internal Agent Helper first created a verified training corpus and guarded customer trust.

Identity Verification (KYC)

Decoupled from single-vendor OCR to direct database lookups

Signal: Vendor downtime was causing deposit drop-offs during live events. Introducing multi-vendor fallback and direct government records cut turnaround from 24h to <1 min.

VIP Loyalty Architecture

Created a 3-tier milestone ladder instead of static perks

Signal: 200+ interviews showed players had no goal after qualifying for VIP. Introducing ₹5K → ₹50K → ₹100K wagering targets unlocked +75% ARPU expansion.

Internal Tooling (GrowthTap)

Built internal analytics instead of buying commercial SaaS

Signal: External platforms cost $50K+/year while restricting bespoke high-frequency game telemetry. Building on Kafka & ClickHouse delivered real-time funnels at fraction of the cost.
