1. The Problem & Market Gap

Small and medium business owners lose up to 30% of their prospective revenue simply due to unanswered inbound phone calls during peak hours or after business hours. Existing customer service solutions fell into two ineffective extremes:

  • Traditional IVR Menus: "Press 1 for Sales, Press 2 for Support" led to a staggering 58% caller drop-off rate before reaching a human.
  • Text-Based Chatbots: Required customers to open a browser and type out queries, which failed completely for customers calling on mobile devices while driving or on the move.

The market needed an autonomous conversational voice assistant that could answer questions, understand accents, qualify leads, and schedule appointments naturally over the telephone without sounding like a robotic synthesizer.

2. Customer Discovery: 200+ User Interviews

Before building a single wireframe or prompt, I conducted 200+ qualitative discovery calls with business operators across service industries (clinics, automotive service, boutique consulting, and real estate).

The Decisive Willingness-to-Pay Signal
When presented with two product prototypes—an intelligent website text chatbot vs. an autonomous phone-answering voice agent—business owners demonstrated a ~3x higher willingness-to-pay for voice automation. A missed chat felt like a minor inconvenience; a missed phone call was a verified lost sale worth hundreds of dollars.

This single discovery insight pivoted our entire product roadmap: we killed the web chat MVP and committed 100% of our engineering bandwidth to building real-time voice intelligence.

3. Technical Architecture & Product Specs

In Voice AI, user experience is strictly bound by end-to-end conversational latency. If an agent pauses for more than 1,000ms after a caller speaks, the human perception of natural conversation collapses.

I collaborated closely with our engineering lead to architect a sub-800ms pipeline:

  • Streaming Speech-to-Text (STT): Employed chunked audio streaming to begin transcription while the caller was still finishing their sentence.
  • Domain-Specific RAG Retrieval: Built a vector-indexed knowledge repository grounded in business FAQs, operating hours, and service pricing.
  • Streaming LLM Inference with Early Token Synthesis: Connected the LLM stream directly to the text-to-speech (TTS) engine, generating voice audio on the first tokens before the full response was generated.
  • Strict Guardrail Boundaries: Formulated structured negative prompt constraints and fallback escalation pathways for medical, financial, or ungrounded queries.

4. Pod Leadership & 4-Week MVP Delivery

As Product Manager, I led an 8-member cross-functional pod (4 Backend/AI Engineers, 2 Product Designers, 2 QA Engineers) working in rapid 1-week sprint cycles.

To hit our aggressive 4-week launch deadline, I enforced ruthless MVP prioritization:

  • Week 1 (Scoping & Prompts): Defined PRDs, designed core conversation flows, and evaluated LLM prompt variants across 50 simulated customer personas.
  • Week 2 (Core Engine): Delivered the telephony integration (Twilio / WebRTC) and baseline streaming STT-LLM-TTS loop.
  • Week 3 (Evaluation Harness): Built automated evaluation test suites checking task completion rate, hallucination rate, and interruption handling.
  • Week 4 (Beta Launch): Onboarded the first cohort of 25 businesses across 100+ languages and dialects.

6. AI Guardrails & Future Horizon

The Critical Lesson in GenAI Product Management: Building an AI demo is trivial; building an AI product that business owners trust with their real customers requires obsessive attention to failure modes and fallback design.

We established a rule: whenever the agent's confidence score dipped below 75%, it never improvised. Instead, it acknowledged the limitation with empathy, gathered caller details, and triggered an instant SMS notification to the business owner with a full call transcript. This guardrail transformed potential customer frustration into verified leads.

This foundation is now being leveraged to scale Lavola.ai, our enterprise outbound lead-generation and appointment scheduling voice platform.
