1. The Opportunity & The Original Vault Hypothesis

Professionals and small business owners constantly deal with meetings, tasks, deadlines, renewals, documents, customer calls, and follow-ups. A traditional human personal assistant can help manage these responsibilities—but hiring a dedicated full-time assistant is neither practical nor affordable for the majority of people.

We saw an opportunity to build an AI-powered personal assistant that could understand users through natural conversation and proactively help them manage their personal and professional lives. However, our initial hypothesis was completely different from what we ended up building.

The Original Hypothesis: A Conversational Document Vault
Hypothesis: "People need a secure place to store their important documents (passports, driving licenses, insurance policies, certificates, and business filings) and retrieve information from them through conversation."

Initial Mental Model: Upload → Understand → Ask → Retrieve
Instead of opening files, users would ask: "What's my passport number?" or "When does my car insurance expire?" and the AI would retrieve the exact values from encrypted storage.

We built and validated an early MVP around this concept. But during validation, we observed a critical truth: document retrieval was not the primary problem users needed solved.

2. Customer Discovery: Uncovering the Real Need

Rather than pushing harder on a failing assumption, I initiated extensive direct user research. Across the lifecycle of MySmartAssistant, I conducted and analyzed approximately 500 customer interviews, calls, and feedback conversations.

A clear, recurring pattern surfaced across busy professionals and SMB operators:

  • They weren't struggling with finding information in files.
  • They were struggling with remembering and executing everything they had to do.
  • They were forgetting meetings, missing policy renewals, dropping client follow-up calls, and losing track of daily task priorities.
  • They were managing multiple responsibilities simultaneously without the budget for full-time administrative staff.
The Fundamental Product Thesis Pivot
From: "Help users retrieve information from documents."
To: "Help users manage their lives and work like an executive personal assistant would."

We redefined MyS as an actionable conversational AI personal assistant. Instead of opening multiple screens or apps, users could simply tell MyS:

  • 💬 "Remind me about my insurance renewal next month."
  • 💬 "I have a meeting with Rahul tomorrow at 3 PM."
  • 💬 "Remind me to call the client right after lunch."
  • 💬 "What tasks do I have left today?"

3. MVP Strategy: Start Where Users Already Are (WhatsApp → Web)

When building a 0→1 consumer AI tool, distribution and habit friction kill adoption faster than technical limitations. Forcing users to download a brand-new app from the App Store to test an unproven assistant creates extreme top-of-funnel drop-off.

I made the strategic product decision to launch the MVP directly on WhatsApp.

The Zero-Friction Validation Hypothesis
"The lower the interaction friction, the easier it is to validate whether users actually want an AI assistant in their daily lives."

On WhatsApp, users could chat with MyS, set reminders, manage schedules, create tasks, and retrieve document data without creating a new login or learning unfamiliar UI patterns. Once we saw high retention and validated core utility, we systematically expanded the product architecture:

Phase 1
WhatsApp Conversational MVP
Validated core assistant behavior, habit loops, and reminder trigger patterns with minimal user onboarding friction.
Phase 2
Native Android & iOS Applications
Built dedicated mobile experiences enabling deeper OS-level reminders, background notifications, and rich assistant capabilities.
Phase 3
Web Dashboard Workspace
Provided desktop-first access for professionals managing extensive task lists, documents, and business schedules.

4. MyS Live: Solving Interaction Effort with Voice AI

As user numbers grew, our analytics revealed an unexpected UX barrier. The product had dedicated, well-designed modules for Reminders, Tasks, Documents, and Schedules—yet users weren't updating them consistently.

Through user interviews, we diagnosed the root cause: The problem was not feature discoverability; the problem was interaction effort. Busy professionals on the move didn't have the patience to open their phones, navigate to a form, and type out 25 words to create a reminder.

The Core Interaction Shift: Typing → Talking
Instead of typing: "Please remind me tomorrow at 10 AM to call the insurance company," the user should simply be able to speak it naturally. We introduced MyS Live—a real-time voice-based assistant experience.

Building a truly natural Voice AI experience required solving complex product and engineering challenges:

  • Response Latency: In voice, any pause longer than 800–1,000ms feels broken. We engineered a streaming STT → LLM inference → TTS pipeline that synthesizes audio on the very first tokens, reducing conversational latency to under 800ms.
  • Barge-In Handling (Interruption): Humans don't wait passively for an assistant to finish long monologues. If MyS says: "Your policy expires on—" and the user interjects: "Wait, which policy?", the system must immediately stop audio playback, preserve context, and process the interruption gracefully.
  • Contextual Prompt Optimization: Optimized prompt structures and conversational memory to handle multi-turn disambiguation, colloquial Indian English phrasing, and rapid context switching without hallucination.
  • Conversational UX: The goal wasn't just to build an AI that answers questions. It was to build an AI that acts and behaves like an executive human assistant.

5. Call Assist & The B2B Enterprise Pivot

During our continuous discovery calls with SMB owners and service professionals, a new pain point surfaced: "What happens when I can't answer my phone?"

Solo practitioners, consultants, and shop owners miss calls constantly when in meetings, driving, or serving existing clients. Every missed call is a lost business opportunity or frustrated customer. Users asked:

The Breakthrough Customer Request
"If MyS can act like my personal assistant, why can't it answer my incoming phone calls when I'm unavailable?"

To solve this, we built Call Assist:

Flow
Customer Calls User → Conditional Call Forwarding → MyS Voice AI Representative
The AI answers naturally, understands the caller's intent, answers business/service queries using grounding knowledge, logs details, and sends an instant SMS summary to the owner.

This expanded MyS from a personal productivity assistant into an autonomous AI voice representative.

The B2B Opportunity: From 1 Assistant → Fleet of Voice Agents

As we demonstrated Call Assist, commercial clients asked: "Can we use this exact voice technology to handle our entire company's customer support and outbound calling?"

This triggered our largest strategic pivot: From B2C Personal Assistant to a dedicated B2B Voice AI Platform:

  • Inbound Voice Agents: Replaced robotic IVRs with intelligent conversational receptionists capable of answering FAQs, checking real-time data via API webhooks, and routing high-value leads.
  • Outbound Calling Campaigns: Enabled businesses to launch automated calling workflows for appointment confirmations, payment reminders, lead qualification, and customer follow-ups.
  • Omnichannel CRM Layer: Expanded beyond voice into customer communication across WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger, laying the foundation for a unified AI customer communications infrastructure.

6. Growth Levers: Onboarding & Subscription Funnel Optimization

While building out core AI capabilities, I tracked the user acquisition funnel to identify monetization leakage. The data revealed two major drop-off bottlenecks:

Bottleneck 1
Subscription Page Ambiguity
Users hesitated at payment due to unclear recurring autopay vs one-time billing
Bottleneck 2
Onboarding Fatigue
Multi-step tutorial carousels caused 28% drop-off before reaching first assistant value

The Product Interventions:

  • Subscription Page Redesign: Rebuilt the pricing screen with complete transparency: explicit monthly vs. annual toggles, clear autopay indicators, guaranteed cancellation controls, and highlight of core value caps.
  • Progressive Onboarding: Stripped out mandatory multi-screen tutorials. Users were dropped directly into the conversational interface within 15 seconds. Deeper features were introduced contextually by the assistant when relevant to the user's conversation.

8. Key Product Decisions & What Made This a 0→1 Journey

The true role of a Product Manager in a 0→1 journey is not writing PRDs for a predetermined roadmap; it is detecting behavioral signals, making tough trade-offs, and steering the product toward real commercial value:

Challenge Observed User Signal Product Decision & Rationale
Document Retrieval Users weren't looking for document search; they struggled to track renewals, tasks, and meetings. Pivoted to AI Personal Assistant: Shifted product thesis from storage to daily life and schedule management.
Typing Friction Users abandoned form-based reminders because typing on mobile was high-effort while busy. Built MyS Live (Voice AI): Replaced "Typing" with "Talking" for hands-free, natural task creation.
Missed Phone Calls Professionals lost business when unable to answer client phone calls while working. Built Call Assist: Engineered call forwarding to an autonomous MyS Voice AI representative.
B2B Enterprise Inquiries Small businesses wanted to automate their own customer calls using our voice engine. Launched B2B Voice AI: Built inbound support agents and outbound calling campaigns for businesses.
Onboarding Drop-off Users dropped during multi-step tutorials before experiencing first assistant value. Progressive Onboarding: Removed mandatory tutorials; let users chat within 15 seconds of install.
Subscription Friction Users hesitated due to confusion over recurring autopay vs one-time billing terms. Transparent Pricing UI: Clarified billing cadence, cancellation terms, and autopay status.
Fragmented Channels Businesses engaged customers on WhatsApp, Instagram, and FB, not just telephone calls. Omnichannel CRM Layer: Expanded platform toward a unified multi-channel AI communications layer.

9. PM Learnings & The "AI that Acts" Philosophy

The biggest paradigm shift in MySmartAssistant was moving from "AI that Answers" to "AI that Acts":

❌ AI That Answers (Chatbot)
User: "When does my car insurance expire?"
AI: "Your policy expires on June 20, 2026."
Passive retrieval. The user gets the answer but still carries the entire burden of remembering to renew and follow up.
✅ AI That Acts (True Assistant)
User: "When does my car insurance expire?"
AI: "Your policy expires on June 20. Would you like me to set a reminder on May 20, and pull comparison quotes for you?"
Active assistance. The AI proactively anticipates needs, removes cognitive load, and executes follow-ups.

5 Core PM Learnings from this 0→1 Journey:

  1. Don't fall in love with the initial idea: The original document vault was an interesting technical concept, but it wasn't the urgent user problem. The initial hypothesis is never the product; the user problem is.
  2. Convert feedback into product signals: Individual user requests are noise; repeating patterns across ~500 customer calls are commercial opportunities.
  3. Conversational AI demands a new definition of UX: Traditional UX is Screens → Buttons → Forms → Navigation. Conversational AI UX is Intent → Context → Conversation → Response → Action. The conversation IS the interface.
  4. Voice AI is about conversational cadence, not just speech-to-text: Turn-taking, latency under 800ms, and natural barge-in handling determine whether an AI feels like an intelligent human or an annoying IVR machine.
  5. The best B2B products often hide inside B2C user behavior: We never set out to build a B2B voice platform; our B2C users showed us that the technology could solve an urgent commercial problem for their businesses.
Final Product Takeaway
"Don't build the product you imagined in a room. Build the product users reveal to you in the market."
