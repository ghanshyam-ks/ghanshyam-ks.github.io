How Customer Discovery Guided 4 Pivots to Build a Multi-Platform GenAI Voice Business

Hypothesized an encrypted document vault ("Ask your passport/policy"); launched MVP on WhatsApp to kill app-install friction and validate habit formation.

200+ user interviews revealed users didn't struggle finding files; they struggled managing tasks and missed calls. Businesses had 3× higher willingness-to-pay for voice vs chat.

Shipped 4-week MVP with 8-member pod → Built MySA Live Voice AI (sub-800ms streaming + barge-in) → Built Call Assist → Scaled into a B2B Voice Platform.

2. The Core PM Philosophy: "AI That Answers" vs "AI That Acts"

The biggest product breakthrough was elevating MySA from a passive chatbot into an active executive assistant that anticipates next steps:

The Chatbot Trap (Reactive)

"AI That Answers"

User: "When does my car insurance expire?"
AI: "Your policy expires on June 20, 2026."
  • Passive fact retrieval; zero follow-up.
  • User carries entire mental burden of remembering to renew.
  • Low user habit formation and high churn.
Low Retention & Utility
The MySA Experience (Proactive)

"AI That Acts"

User: "When does my car insurance expire?"
AI: "Your policy expires on June 20. Would you like me to set a reminder on May 20 and pull comparison quotes for you?"
  • Anticipates needs and eliminates cognitive load.
  • Proactively handles reminders, calendar events, and follow-ups.
  • Creates indispensable daily executive utility.
+15% Retention Lift

3. Voice AI UX: Solving Latency & Barge-In

A voice assistant feels broken if response latency exceeds 1 second, or if the user cannot interrupt mid-sentence. We engineered a seamless real-time loop:

Real-Time Streaming Voice AI Pipeline
Achieved sub-800ms end-to-end response time with natural conversational cadence:
1. Streaming STT
Transcribes chunked audio while user is still speaking.
2. Contextual LLM
RAG-grounded retrieval; strict guardrails against hallucination.
3. Early Token TTS
Synthesizes audio on first output tokens; <800ms latency.
4. Real-Time Barge-In
Instantly halts audio when user interrupts mid-sentence.

4. Growth Levers: Eliminating Drop-Off in Onboarding

Funnel telemetry identified two major conversion drop-offs: tutorial fatigue and subscription pricing ambiguity.

The Leaky Funnel (Before)

High Cognitive Friction

  • 5-Screen Tutorial: 28% drop-off before reaching first assistant value.
  • Billing Confusion: Uncertainty over recurring autopay vs one-time.
  • Delayed Activation: Users forced to configure settings before chatting.
28% Drop-Off at Onboarding
The Optimized Funnel (After)

Progressive Value Delivery

  • 15-Second Time-to-Value: Removed tutorials; dropped straight into chat.
  • Transparent Pricing UI: Crystal-clear monthly/annual toggles & autopay.
  • In-Context Learning: Assistant introduces features when relevant.
+15% Retention Improvement

5. Key Product Decisions & Behavioral Signals

Challenge Observed User Signal Product Decision & Rationale
Document Retrieval Users didn't search docs; struggled to track renewals, meetings, and tasks. Pivoted to Personal Assistant: Shifted focus from storage to daily life management.
App Download Barrier High friction downloading a new app to test an unproven assistant. Launched on WhatsApp: Zero-friction MVP to validate habit formation.
Typing Friction Users avoided manual form entries on mobile while on the move. Built MySA Live (Voice AI): Replaced typing with natural conversational voice.
Missed Phone Calls Users lost deals when unable to answer customer calls while busy. Built Call Assist: Conditional call forwarding to MySA AI representative.
B2B Inquiries Businesses requested to use voice engine for customer support & sales. Launched B2B Voice AI: Built inbound agents & outbound campaign platform.
Onboarding Drop-off Users dropped during multi-step tutorials before experiencing value. Progressive Onboarding: Let users chat within 15 seconds of install.
Subscription Friction Users hesitated due to confusion over recurring autopay vs one-time. Transparent Pricing UI: Clarified billing cadence and cancellation terms.
