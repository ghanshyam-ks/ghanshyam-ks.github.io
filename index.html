₹3L → ₹8L/day

The Challenge

Rocket Skill was one of Witzeal's core revenue drivers, operating at 30K–40K DAU. However, daily revenue had stagnated at approximately ₹3L/day. Players were clustering around low-entry-fee tables, high-value players experienced table liquidity issues, and the game economy was not structured to encourage natural progression.

Discovery & Insight

I started with the economy, not the feature list. Analyzing gameplay telemetry, user drop-offs, and competitor mechanics revealed three root causes:

The Three Structural Bottlenecks
1. Table Clutter: 8+ entry fee tiers diluted liquidity, leading to long wait times and abandoned sessions.
2. Flat Prize Pools: Top players saw diminishing marginal reward for entering higher-stake tables.
3. Absence of Re-engagement Hooks: When high-intent users lost a round, there was no structured next-step offer to keep them playing.

What I Learned

Monetization is an economy design problem, not just a pricing problem. You cannot simply raise fees or add promotional popups; you must balance player risk perception, liquidity across tiers, and house margin. The largest gains came from streamlining the table catalog to create higher table density rather than adding more options.

The Core Problem

The original VIP club had a flat entry requirement: wager ₹5,000 in 30 days. Once a player achieved this threshold, there was no higher tier to target. High-rollers had no additional goals, causing wagering velocity to plateau.

What 200+ VIP Interviews Revealed
• Lack of Awareness: A substantial portion of eligible players didn't even realize they had unlocked VIP status.
• Weak Utility: The existing perks were perceived as uninspiring; players who sought VIP only did so for the table badge.
• No Forward Momentum: Without higher milestones, post-VIP retention dropped sharply after day 15.
Redesigned 3-Tier Wagering Progression Architecture Milestone-Driven
Tier 1 · VIP
₹5,000 Wager
VIP Badge on table, standard cashback, priority email response.
Tier 2 · VIP Plus
₹50,000 Wager
Gold border, elevated rakeback, expedited instant withdrawals, dedicated line.
Tier 3 · VIP Pro
₹100,000 Wager
Elite Diamond badge, personal VIP relationship manager, custom high-stake tables.
Execution Strategy

To drive progression, we introduced behavioral milestone nudges. When a player reached 60% of their next tier's wagering requirement (e.g., ₹30,000 toward VIP Plus), the app triggered targeted personalized notifications showing remaining progress and the specific unlocks awaiting them.

What I Learned

Loyalty programs fail when they treat status as a destination rather than a journey. By giving players an aspirational ladder with clear milestones and immediate progress visibility, we converted passive users into active spenders striving for the next tier.

Technical Innovation

The hidden bottleneck was Indian names. Standard OCR models compared exact text strings. We implemented a multi-stage fuzzy name-matching logic calibrated specifically for Indian naming conventions (handling honorifics like Kumar/Singh, swapped surnames, and abbreviations). Methodology

My product operating system

A structured framework for moving from ambiguous user signals to repeatable business outcomes.

01

Discover

Talk directly to users (2,000+ interviews), analyze funnel drop-offs, benchmark competitors, and map the underlying economic or operational system.

02

Frame

Separate symptoms from root problems. Define the true user friction, business constraints, and north-star outcomes before writing a single requirement.

03

Prioritize

Use RICE scoring and willingness-to-pay signals to focus on the smallest viable MVP rather than attempting to ship exhaustive backlogs.

04

Experiment

Formulate hypotheses with explicit leading and lagging indicators. Run live A/B iterations to balance margins, engagement, and conversion.

05

Ship & Learn

Deploy with comprehensive telemetry, monitor quality metrics and edge-case exceptions, conduct retrospectives, and double down on validated drivers.

Strategic Judgement

How I make product trade-offs

Strong product management is about what you choose not to do. Real trade-offs and the signals that drove them.

Voice vs Chat Automation

Prioritized voice for MVP instead of chat

Signal: 200+ user discovery calls revealed small businesses had ~3x higher willingness-to-pay for voice calling because unanswered phone calls meant direct lost revenue.

AI Support Sequencing

Empowered agents with AI before automating customers

Signal: Agent knowledge was the true bottleneck, not channel capacity. Deploying an internal Agent Helper first created a verified training corpus and guarded customer trust.

Identity Verification (KYC)

Decoupled from single-vendor OCR to direct database lookups

Signal: Vendor downtime was causing deposit drop-offs during live events. Introducing multi-vendor fallback and direct government records cut turnaround from 24h to <1 min.

VIP Loyalty Architecture

Created a 3-tier milestone ladder instead of static perks

Signal: 200+ interviews showed players had no goal after qualifying for VIP. Introducing ₹5K → ₹50K → ₹100K wagering targets unlocked +75% ARPU expansion.

Internal Tooling (GrowthTap)

Built internal analytics instead of buying commercial SaaS

Signal: External platforms cost $50K+/year while restricting bespoke high-frequency game telemetry. Building on Kafka & ClickHouse delivered real-time funnels at fraction of the cost.
