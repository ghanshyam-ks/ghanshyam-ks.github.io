Voice vs Chat Automation

❌ Conventional: Build standard text chatbot app.
✓ Ghanshyam's Move: Prioritize voice calling MVP first.
Signal: ~500 user calls proved SMBs had ~3x higher willingness-to-pay for voice because missed calls meant direct lost revenue.

CRM · AI SEQUENCING

AI Support Sequencing

❌ Conventional: Expose raw AI bot to end-users.
✓ Ghanshyam's Move: Deploy AI Copilot to human agents first.
Signal: Agent knowledge was true bottleneck; internal helper trained verified corpus and eliminated hallucination risk.
Impact: -45% FRT · CSAT 2/5 → 4.2/5

KYC Architecture
