Strategic Judgement

How I Make Product Trade-Offs

Strong product management is about what you choose not to do. Real trade-offs and the signals that drove them.

Voice vs Chat Automation

Prioritized voice for MVP instead of chat

Signal: 200+ user discovery calls revealed small businesses had ~3x higher willingness-to-pay for voice calling because unanswered phone calls meant direct lost revenue.

AI Support Sequencing

Empowered agents with AI before automating customers

Signal: Agent knowledge was the true bottleneck, not channel capacity. Deploying an internal Agent Helper first created a verified training corpus and guarded customer trust.

Identity Verification (KYC)

Decoupled from single-vendor OCR to direct database lookups

Signal: Vendor downtime was causing deposit drop-offs during live events. Introducing multi-vendor fallback and direct government records cut turnaround from 24h to <1 min.
