VIP Club: Turning a static badge into a progression system

Problem: 200+ qualitative interviews revealed high-wagering players had no post-qualification goals, causing plateaued wagering.

Solution: Restructured into a 3-tier wagering progression ladder (₹5K / ₹50K / ₹100K) with milestone nudges and personalized loss-back credits.

Outcome: Grew active VIP base from 45K to 65K (+44%) and lifted average VIP ARPU from ₹8K to ₹14K (+75%).
